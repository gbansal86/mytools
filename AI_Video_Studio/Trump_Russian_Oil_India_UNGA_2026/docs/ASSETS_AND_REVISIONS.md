# Source asset and revision recipe

The full-resolution AI-generated source artwork (3 images), expanded 10-scene source renderer, scene manifest, draft-voice stems and thumbnail are preserved in the **downloadable source ZIP provided in the conversation**. Binary AI images and the 2m51s MP4 have **not** been uploaded to this GitHub branch. The repository renderer supports a missing-image fallback and remains editable.

## To regenerate the opening illustration
Create a 16:9 cinematic **editorial collage** featuring three separate portraits: Donald Trump on the left, Narendra Modi in the center, Vladimir Putin on the right. Use a UN General Assembly hall **only as a conceptual backdrop**, with a fictional stylized 3D globe and oil tanker across the foreground. There must be **no handshake, no shared podium, no claim of a real three-way UN meeting**. Do NOT add falsely attributed speech bubbles or captions. Keep the bottom third clear for a title. Save the user-approved result as `assets/ai_un_composite.png`.

Optional thumbnail variant: increase separation between the portraits, high-contrast night-sky / cobalt-blue and amber lighting, 3D tanker route from Russia to India. Keep exact geographic labels and text out of the generative model; add those in the compositor using verifiable text.

## Three-second image-to-video prompt for an illustration
“Animate the editorial 3D background only: slow parallax camera push, independently moving oil tanker on the ocean with a natural wake, subtle light reflections and soft depth-of-field; retain facial identity and expression as a **still illustrated montage**, no lip sync, no head turning or gestures implying a recorded real event. Do not add any text, flags, logos, fake recording timestamps or additional people. Produce 3 seconds, 16:9, 24/30 fps.”

## Factual motion-graphics layers
Use the compositor for the Russia → India route, US tariff-authority decision tree and signed → discussed → applied? sequence. AI-drawn maps are not evidence of geography. Add exact names, rates, dates and source captions in editable vector/text layers **after** generating visual backgrounds.

## Revision controls
The editable manifest tracks each scene's narration and duration. Regenerate selected scenes, then compile all MP4s in order and remux checked voice/music audio. If you change the policy status after 24 September 2026, update the US law card, the branch of the tariff flowchart, the source register, captions and voiceover together.

**Publication gate:** replace the offline synthetic draft speech with a natural and licensed recording, visually review every frame, verify all official quotations and carry proper AI disclosure.
