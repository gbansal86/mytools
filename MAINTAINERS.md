# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

- [@gbansal86](https://github.com/gbansal86) — repository owner and primary maintainer.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The primary maintainer is responsible for:

- reviewing and merging pull requests;
- triaging reproducible issues and support reports;
- maintaining repository and tool documentation;
- keeping CI and dependency-maintenance configuration healthy;
- reviewing security/privacy-sensitive changes;
- preparing and documenting releases;
- maintaining licensing and third-party attribution;
- deciding whether new utilities fit the repository's scope;
- preserving the project's safety-first behavior around local files and system changes.

Substantive outside contributions are welcome and should follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Additional maintainers may be added after sustained, high-quality contributions and demonstrated familiarity with the affected tools, testing expectations, security/privacy rules, and review standards. Maintainer status should reflect real ongoing responsibility rather than a title added for appearance.
