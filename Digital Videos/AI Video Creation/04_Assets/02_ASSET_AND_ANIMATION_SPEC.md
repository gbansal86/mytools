# 02 — Visual system and real-motion animation specification

## Portrait canvas
1080×1920; protect top ~220 px and bottom ~320 px from essential labels and controls; put learning labels near center; use 1–2 short lines of captions, never a full infographic scaled to fit. White/off-white canvas, navy labels, blue data lines, orange accent, green verified state; color should never be the sole meaning cue. Text and arrows are editor-rendered, not baked into generated illustrations.

## Assets to create and retain separately
- Eight uncluttered scene backgrounds (or one reused background), with no embedded words.
- Vector text/image/audio/numbers tiles and arrow paths; central data container; network metaphor; email types; city/map and route polylines; outputs (text/image/music/code); verification checklist; four final mind-map nodes.
- Editable label layers and linked voice cue list; original prompts/seeds/settings if AI art is used.
- The earlier infographic and 3D mind-map concept pictures may inform palette/layout, but a flattened PNG alone cannot provide separately moving internal objects. Redraw/extract elements as editable assets; verify permissions.

## Motion contract (must be visible in rendered frames)
| Scene | Moving objects | Annotations | Failure to catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook icons enter from opposite sides | question builds | all elements appear at once |
| 2 | four tiles physically follow individual paths into data node | tile name appears with each arrival | static image with camera zoom only |
| 3 | example cards feed network, connections pulse progressively | “training” then “patterns” | implies thinking human brain |
| 4 | emails traverse two branches; uncertain case marked | spam / genuine / likely | no traceable path |
| 5 | traffic segments change, route path draws from A to B | estimated time, not guaranteed | wrong/contradictory time math |
| 6 | prompt enters; output objects emerge and fan out | generate text/image/music/code | output existed before prompt |
| 7 | claim gets a visible source check | verify before relying | green check without any verification |
| 8 | view zooms out to four-node map; paths draw in learning order | readable four-stage recap | text too small on phone |

## Animation approach
Use a timeline editor/Blender or SVG motion layers to keyframe icon coordinates, opacity, arrow stroke reveal, glow and camera movement. Drive scene lengths from approved spoken-word timestamps rather than guessed image duration. Export scene renders for inspection before assembling. Add per-scene alt explanations and captions.

## Rights and audit
Document who created each image/clip, which model/tool and version, prompt, date, permission/license and any borrowed source. Do not push user-provided private video or cookies to a public repo. Only publish assets whose reuse rights are confirmed. Generated AI visuals are not automatically proof of originality or unrestricted commercial reuse.
