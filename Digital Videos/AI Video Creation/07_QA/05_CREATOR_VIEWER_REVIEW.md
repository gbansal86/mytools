# Creator lens vs first-time viewer lens — 2026-09-24

## Creator lens (principles, not an imitation of any individual creator)
**Question:** Why would someone stay through the first three seconds and understand it without sound?
- Open with the paradox “AI answers quickly; it learned earlier,” instead of a vague title card.
- Reveal one thing at a time, with constant visual change that *explains* the point rather than distracting.
- Make every graphic trace a cause-and-effect action: examples move into training; new input moves into the trained model; output comes out.
- Keep the format recognizable from episode to episode: off-white canvas, navy titles, orange for learning, blue for input, green for using, red for review.
- Do not overload this first episode with traffic maps, recipes, and all of generative AI. One spam example carries the idea, with one short generative-AI connection.

## Viewer lens: five-second memory test
- “Why should I care?” The hook promises an answer to how instant AI answers are possible.
- “What did I learn?” I can distinguish *training* from *using a trained model*.
- “Can I read it?” Full poster text is removed; concise center-safe labels survive a mobile-screen test.
- “Can I follow the arrows?” All moving paths have meaningful sources/destinations and appear in narrated order.
- “What would confuse me?” A brain icon that suggests a biological brain; a training arrow directly into a user's query; red/green implying perfect certainty; claims that ALL AI constantly learns from every user interaction. The revision corrects or qualifies these.

## First pass → second pass decisions
1. Replace broad “all of AI” with **training vs use**, a clear single learning outcome.
2. Use the same spam-email example across training and inference to make continuity visible.
3. Include an explicit *likely* label and a human-check ending, not an unconditional correct-answer checkmark.
4. Make source artwork decorative only. Recreate moving shapes, labels and arrows in editable layers so text is crisp.
5. Render a **silent 60-second motion animatic** first to test visual causality. Its timings remain provisional until voice is actually recorded and synced.
6. Final 3D branch animation and natural voice are quality gates, not fabricated “done” milestones.
