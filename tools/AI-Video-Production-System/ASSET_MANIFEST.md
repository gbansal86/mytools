## Core editing lesson

All topic visuals must be triggered from the **final edited audio timeline**, not from raw-source timestamps. This prevents:
- images appearing before the subject is spoken
- subtitles drifting after edits
- airbase/hangar imagery appearing during the preceding cessation discussion
- closing graphics appearing before the actual closing line
