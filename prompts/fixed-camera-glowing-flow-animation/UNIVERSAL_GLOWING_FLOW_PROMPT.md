# Universal Fixed-Camera Glowing Flow Animation Prompt

Use this with any still image when you want internal glowing-flow animation without moving the camera.

## Master Prompt

Animate this image while preserving the original composition.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the camera completely stationary.
- Do NOT pan, zoom, crop, rotate, shake, or move the whole image.
- Preserve the original objects, text, icons, faces, layout, colors, and background.
- Do not regenerate or redesign the image unless necessary.
- Motion should happen INSIDE the image.

Analyze the image first and identify natural paths, connections, outlines, roads, wires, branches, borders, streams, arrows, networks, or relationships that can support animated flow.

Create elegant glowing energy/data flows along those natural paths.

FLOW EFFECT:
- Start each flow with a small light pulse.
- Make a bright energy particle travel smoothly along the path.
- Leave a soft neon glowing trail behind it.
- Add smaller particles following the main pulse.
- Use bloom and soft light around active paths.
- Let nearby surfaces subtly reflect the moving light.
- Make the destination/object gently illuminate when the energy reaches it.
- Previously activated paths may remain softly illuminated.
- Use colors that complement the original image rather than forcing predetermined colors.

MOTION:
- Smooth and controlled.
- Premium cinematic appearance.
- No random movement.
- No excessive flashing.
- No rapid animation.
- No distracting particle overload.
- Do not obscure important text or subjects.

SEQUENCE:
When multiple paths or objects exist, activate them one at a time in a logical visual sequence.

At the end, allow all activated paths to glow together briefly and create a subtle final pulse through the overall image.

DEPTH:
Where appropriate, add subtle atmospheric particles, light reflections, volumetric glow, depth and ambient illumination while preserving the original design.

The animation should feel as though energy, information, electricity, light, or data is naturally travelling through the original image.

The viewer should feel that the still image has come alive—not that the camera is moving across a still picture.

If the image does not contain obvious paths, intelligently identify meaningful relationships between important objects and create tasteful curved flow paths connecting them.

Do not change the meaning or content of the original image.

## Short trigger phrase

**Fixed-Camera Glowing Flow Animation**

Key rule: **animate only the image contents; keep the camera 100% stationary.**
