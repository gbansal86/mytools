# Code of Conduct

MyTools welcomes constructive participation from people with different experience levels.

Participants should communicate respectfully, focus criticism on code or ideas, use reproducible evidence where possible, make space for beginner questions, avoid harassment/threats/discrimination/personal attacks, respect privacy, and disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a contribution.

Technical disagreement is normal. Prefer tests, documentation, source references, and clearly stated trade-offs over hostility.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.

For non-sensitive moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through an appropriate GitHub issue or profile contact channel. Do not publish private personal information while reporting a concern.
