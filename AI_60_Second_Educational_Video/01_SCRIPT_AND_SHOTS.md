# 01 — Recording script and shot plan (pilot v0.1)

**Important:** This is a draft to test with an actual voice, not a claim of a finished recording. Use a warm conversational adult voice; natural pauses after the hook, before each example, and at the final takeaway. Do not stretch speech to force it to 60 seconds. Adjust scene boundaries after the real narration is measured.

| Approx. time | Spoken script | On-screen demonstration and word-cued annotation |
|---|---|---|
| 00–05 | "Ever wonder how AI spots spam or makes a picture? Here's the simple idea." | Hook question; two icons move into focus. Annotation: “How does AI work?” |
| 05–12 | "First, we give a model examples: words, images, sounds, or numbers." | Editable text, image, audio, number tiles move along visible curved paths into a data node. Label: “Examples → data.” |

## Audio direction
Natural, friendly, curious; slight emphasis on *examples*, *training*, and *check*. Avoid overly dramatic trailer voice. Record WAV 48 kHz if possible, then normalize and listen on a phone speaker. Keep room tone if human-recorded; do not synthesize the identity of any identifiable speaker without permission.

## Scientific nuance
Training adjusts parameters using examples; not every deployment system continues learning from each user interaction. Spam classification is probabilistic, and routing can use conventional algorithms alongside predictions. AI-generated output can be plausible yet incorrect. On-screen brain/network graphics are metaphors, not a depiction of physical computation.
