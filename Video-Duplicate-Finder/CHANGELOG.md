# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial public release.
- Recursive video discovery from `video_paths.txt`.
- Duration/aspect-ratio candidate filtering.
- 11-point perceptual dHash sampling.
- Nearby timestamp refinement for small intro/outro shifts.
- Fingerprint cache for faster repeat scans.
- CSV and human-readable group reports.
- Local browser review grid with video playback where the browser supports the codec.
- Safe Windows Recycle Bin processing with group-level guard rails.
- Portable FFmpeg installer stored under `_runtime/`.
